## PRODUCTION STEPS

### Step 1 — Research pack (30 min, Day 2)
Run **Prompt 6A** (prompt library) → produces the verified fact sheet: dates, numbers, quotes (chairman, Rob Edwards, the tweet), injury timeline. **Check every number it returns against a source** — AI drafts, you verify. Non-negotiable for a doc.

### Step 2 — Script draft (45 min, Day 2)
Run **Prompt 6B** with the fact pack pasted in → full 1,200–1,400-word narration script in the five-act structure with [STILL]/[CLIP]/[STOCK] markers per paragraph.
Then **Prompt 6C** (the polish pass) → tightens hooks, adds re-hooks every 60–90s, kills clichés.
**Read it aloud once.** Anywhere you stumble, the viewer stumbles.

### Step 3 — Voiceover (30 min, Day 3)
- ElevenLabs free tier → deep male documentary voice (same WC-DOC preset as your channel) → paste script in sections → download MP3s
- Direction: slow on Act 2 (the fall), let sentences breathe; pace up in Act 4

### Step 4 — Assembly (2h, Day 3–4) — CapCut or DaVinci Resolve (both free)
1. Lay narration MP3s end to end = the spine
2. Drop visuals per the act table: your auto-cut clips (each ≤5–7s, per the rights checklist), stills with Ken Burns zooms, stock B-roll connective tissue

4. Music: royalty-free emotional piano/strings (NOT "Red Days" on YouTube) — duck −20dB under narration; full silence for 2 beats at the skull moment
5. Captions ON for the cold open at minimum
6. Export 1080p 16:9
