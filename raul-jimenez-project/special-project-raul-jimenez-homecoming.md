# PART 3 — THE AUTOMATED HIGHLIGHT-CUTTING PIPELINE (deep research)

You asked how to automatically cut real football highlights with AI. Here's the honest, complete picture — the API you found, what it actually gives you, and the free open-source stack that does the cutting.

## 3.1 The Highlightly API (the one you found) — what it really is
**Football Highlights API (Highlightly) on RapidAPI** — verified findings:

- **Free BASIC tier: 100 requests/day, no credit card.** Paid tiers unlock unlimited highlights/leagues.
- **Endpoints:** `GET /highlights`, `GET /matches`, `GET /matches/{id}`, `GET /events`, `GET /lineups/{id}`, `GET /leagues` — filterable by league, team, date, season, with limit/offset pagination.
- **What a highlight object contains:** match metadata (teams, league, season, date), a title/description (often event-level: *"X scores to give Y the lead"*), a thumbnail, a `type` (VERIFIED/UNVERIFIED), and a **`url`**.
- **⚠️ The critical catch:** that `url` is sometimes a **direct .mp4 on a broadcaster CDN** (e.g. ESPN clips for US-covered competitions) but very often just a **link/embed to an external page** (club site, YouTube embed). The API is an *aggregator/indexer* of highlights — it is **not a rights licence** to download and re-edit the footage. Coverage is strongest for recent matches (added 0–48h after games); deep archives from Jiménez's 2018–2023 Wolves era will be patchy.
- **Best uses for YOUR project:**
  1. **Event timeline data** — exact minute-by-minute goal/card events for any Jiménez match → tells your cutter *where* to look in any footage you have.
  2. **Discovery** — programmatically list every Jiménez goal clip that exists and where it lives.
  3. **Live World Cup mode** — during Mexico matches, poll `/events` for "Jiménez" and trigger your reactive workflow within minutes of a goal.

**Free alternatives/complements:**
- **Scorebat Video API** (scorebat.com/video-api) — free JSON feed of embeddable highlight videos for major leagues. Embeds only (great for a website/landing page, not raw files).
- **API-Football / football-data.org free tiers** — fixture + event data (no video) to enrich your timeline metadata.

## 3.2 ⚠️ The rights reality (read before building anything)
Be smart about this — it determines platform strategy:

- Premier League, EFL and World Cup match footage is owned by leagues/broadcasters and is the **most aggressively enforced video content on earth**. On YouTube, Content ID will detect re-used broadcast footage and **claim (revenue redirected), block, or strike** the video. The PL has a dedicated takedown operation.
- **What this means practically, not theoretically:**
  1. **TikTok/Reels tolerate short match clips far more** than YouTube — enforcement exists but is lighter and slower. Short-form built on brief clips is the lower-risk lane.
  2. **On YouTube long-form, transform hard:** short clips (seconds, not sequences), heavy narration over the top, analysis/criticism framing, your own graphics/maps/stat overlays between clips, stills + Ken Burns zooms instead of full-motion where possible. Transformative ≠ immunity, but it massively reduces blocks and makes claims (revenue share) the likely outcome instead of strikes.
  3. **Run this on a secondary channel first** if you're protective of your main channel — never absorb strike risk on your flagship.
  4. **Embeds are always safe:** Scorebat/YouTube embeds on your landing page/newsletter carry zero risk.
  5. Expect claimed videos to be **demonetized for you** — this project's ROI is *audience growth during a perfect-storm story*, not AdSense on the doc.
- **🎵 The music — "Red Days" by Telescreens:** commercial track → Content ID will flag it on YouTube (claim/block) and IG/Facebook can mute. **Use it the smart way:** on TikTok/Reels, add it **from the in-app sound library** if it's there (licensed in-app = safe + you join the sound's discovery page = free distribution). For the YouTube doc, use a royalty-free emotional alternative (see toolbox) or accept the claim consciously. Owning the MP3 ≠ owning sync rights.

## 3.3 The free AI cutting stack (all open-source, all runs on your MacBook)
This is the assembly line: **source video in → finished vertical clips out.**

### Stage A — Sourcing & indexing
| Tool | Role | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Highlightly API** (free tier) | Index every Jiménez goal/event + timestamps; live WC event triggers | Free (100 req/day) |
| **yt-dlp** (github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) | The standard open-source video downloader for sourcing footage you have a basis to work with | Free |
| **ffmpeg** | The engine under everything: cutting, cropping, encoding, audio | Free |

### Stage B — Finding the moments automatically (the "AI cut")
Four detection layers — combined score finds the highlight moments in any match/compilation footage:

1. **🔊 Crowd-audio peak detection** (the classic, shockingly effective trick): compute rolling RMS volume of the audio track; crowd roars = goals/big moments. ~25 lines of Python with moviepy/numpy (the famous Zulko soccer-cuts method). Catches ~90% of goals with zero ML.
2. **🗣️ Commentary keyword spotting — OpenAI Whisper** (free, MIT, runs locally): transcribe commentary with word timestamps → search for "Jiménez", "goal", "scores", "header" → every mention is a candidate moment. This is your **player-specific filter** — the thing that turns "match highlights" into "Jiménez highlights".
3. **🎬 Shot-boundary detection — PySceneDetect** (free): finds camera cuts so your clips start/end on clean frames (broadcast replays = a cut to closeup right after goals; detecting the replay-cluster pattern confirms an event).
4. **🧍 Object detection — YOLOv8/ultralytics** (free): detect players/ball; track the action's position in frame — used in Stage C for smart cropping, and optionally for goal-mouth-action detection.

**Ready-made repos that bundle these (live & working, verified):**
- **Aseiel/VideoHighlighter** — open-source local AI video analyzer: scene + motion + audio peaks + YOLO + action recognition + Whisper transcript analysis → auto-cuts top-scoring segments into highlight clips. Configurable, has a GUI, runs offline. The closest thing to a turnkey "AI highlight cutter" on GitHub.
- **AkhilNam/Sports-Highlight-Detector** — Python, OpenCV + YOLOv8, has a **dedicated soccer mode**; outputs timestamped clips to a folder.
- **Zulko's soccer-cuts script** — the 25-line audio-peak original; simplest possible starting point.

5. **🧠 LLM ranking & packaging (free tiers):** feed clip metadata (transcript snippet, event type, audio-peak score) to **Gemini free tier** or a local **Ollama** model (llama3) → ranks clips by emotional value + writes the hook line and caption for each. Your taste stays in charge; the machine does the drafting.

### Stage C — Auto-formatting for platforms
- **16:9 → 9:16 smart crop:** YOLO detects where players/ball are per frame → ffmpeg crop filter follows the action (or use ffmpeg's `cropdetect`+centre crop as the dumb-but-fine fallback). Open-source "autocrop/reframe" repos exist, but YOLO-centroid → crop window is 30 lines of Python.
- **Captions:** Whisper output → styled subtitles via ffmpeg (or drop the clip into **CapCut desktop, free** for the bouncy karaoke captions that perform on TikTok).
- **Polish/manual pass:** **CapCut** (free) or **DaVinci Resolve** (free, pro-grade) for the hero doc; your MoneyPrinterTurbo stays in the loop ONLY for stock-footage narrative segments (the rights-safe connective tissue between real clips).
- **Music:** TikTok/Reels → "Red Days" via in-app library; YouTube → royalty-free emotional tracks (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, Uppbeat free tier).

### Stage D — Publishing
- **Postiz** (self-hosted, free, unlimited channels) for scheduling the planned slate across TikTok/Shorts/Reels + your multi-account setup.
- **Native uploads for the reactive World Cup clips** (speed + algorithm preference + in-app sounds).
