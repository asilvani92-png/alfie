## World Cup 2026 Edition — Master Plan & Index

---

## 🎯 THE STRATEGY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Each week is built around **one pillar documentary** (7–9 min, YouTube). That single video is atomized into **5–7 Shorts/Reels/TikToks**, **2–3 social engagement posts**, and **1 newsletter**. On top of the planned calendar sits a **daily "Reactive Slot"** — if anything big happens at the World Cup that day, you publish a narrated reaction short within 2–4 hours to ride the algorithmic spike. Days 27–30 convert attention into an **email list** via two lead magnets.

## ⚙️ THE DAILY OPERATING RHYTHM (90–150 min/day)

| Time block | Task |
|---|---|
| **Block 1 (30–45 min)** | Check overnight analytics + WC news scan (reactive slot trigger?) |
| **Block 2 (45–60 min)** | Produce today's planned content (script → MPT → export) |
| **Block 3 (15–30 min)** | Publish at optimal times, write captions, pin a comment |
| **Block 4 (15 min)** | Reply to EVERY comment in the first hour (algorithm fuel) |

### 🚨 The Reactive Slot (every single day)
If a major WC moment happens (upset, red card, viral fan clip, record broken):
1. Write a 120–150 word narration script within 60 minutes.
2. MPT: fast-cut style, urgent voice tone, stock crowd/stadium footage.
3. Publish to TikTok + Shorts within **2–4 hours** of the moment.
4. Title formula: *"What just happened at the World Cup will be talked about for years."*

Reactive content **overrides** the planned short for that day if needed — speed beats schedule.

## 🧭 NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES

1. **Never miss the first 3 seconds.** Every video opens with the hook line — no logos, no intros.
2. **One CTA per asset.** Long-form → subscribe; Shorts → follow; Week 4+ → download the freebie.
3. **No match footage.** Stock B-roll + narration only (Pexels/Pixabay). Zero copyright risk.
4. **Reply to comments within 60 minutes of posting.** Early engagement velocity is the single biggest ranking signal.
5. **Ship at 80% quality.** During a live tournament, timing beats polish.
