### Naming rules used (why these work)
1. **Pronounceable + spellable** — people search what they hear in narration
2. **No numbers/underscores in the primary** — looks like a bot account
3. **Story/doc signal words** (files, papers, tales, stories, docs) — sets the format expectation before the first frame
4. **Nation/club neutral** — never alienate half the audience in a tribal sport
5. **Evergreen** — nothing locks you to WC2026; the brand survives past July
