# 📧 NEWSLETTER TEMPLATES — All 5 Formats, Fill-in-the-Blank

> Platform: any (ConvertKit/Beehiiv/Substack — free tiers fine).
> Cadence in this 30-day plan: Day 07, 12, 19, 26 (+ optional Day 30 recap).
> Golden rule for subject lines: **numbers + emotion**. "The night football almost ended a war" beats "Newsletter #4" every time.

---

## 1️⃣ "THE WEEKLY GOLAZO" (Mondays / Day 07)
**Purpose:** weekly viral-moment roundup → drives YouTube views.

```
SUBJECT: The 5 moments football couldn't stop talking about this week
PREVIEW: #3 made a defender world-famous overnight.

⚽ THE WEEKLY GOLAZO — [date]

This week in 5 moments:

1. 🥇 [Biggest viral moment] — [2-sentence story + why it matters]
2. [Moment 2] — [2 sentences]
3. [Moment 3] — [2 sentences]
4. [Moment 4] — [2 sentences]
5. [Wildcard/funny moment] — [2 sentences]

🎬 ICYMI: My new documentary "[title]" is live.
[1-line tease — the most shocking fact inside]
→ Watch here: [link]

Next week: [one-line tease of the next doc].

— [Your channel name]
P.S. Hit reply: which moment was YOUR #1?
```

---

## 2️⃣ "THE UNTOLD STORY" (Wednesdays / Day 12)
**Purpose:** one deep-dive story, teases the documentary.

```
SUBJECT: The night [country/player] [shocking thing]
PREVIEW: Nobody talks about what happened after the final whistle.

THE UNTOLD STORY — Vol. [n]

[HOOK PARAGRAPH — the cold-open of the story, 2–3 sentences.]

[BODY — 3 short paragraphs: setup → twist → consequence.
 Write it like narration. End on the cliffhanger.]

The full story — what the cameras never showed — is in this week's
documentary: → [link]

— [Channel name]
```

---

## 3️⃣ "WORLD CUP WATCH GUIDE" (match weeks / Day 19)
**Purpose:** utility → saves, forwards, loyalty.

```
SUBJECT: 3 matches you cannot miss this week (one is a trap)
PREVIEW: Plus my prediction that will probably age terribly.

🗓️ THIS WEEK'S MUST-WATCH

1. [Team A] vs [Team B] — [day/time UK + US ET]
   Why: [1 line]. My call: [score].
2. [match 2] — Why: [1 line]. My call: [score].
3. [match 3] — ⚠️ The trap game: [why the favourite might fall].

📊 STAT OF THE WEEK: [one surprising stat]
🎬 NEW VIDEO: [title] → [link]

Forward this to your match-day group chat. They'll thank you.
```

---

## 4️⃣ "BEHIND THE DOCUMENTARY" (Fridays / optional)
**Purpose:** trust + community; shows the craft (faceless ≠ anonymous).

```
SUBJECT: I spent [X] hours on one 8-minute video. Here's what I found.
PREVIEW: Including the fact I had to cut.

BEHIND THE DOCUMENTARY

This week's video: "[title]" → [link]

🔍 The rabbit hole: [what you researched, 2–3 sentences]
😲 The fact that didn't make the cut: [exclusive tidbit — newsletter-only]
🛠️ One thing I changed in my process: [1–2 sentences]

Reply and tell me: what should I investigate next?
```

---

## 5️⃣ "THE FAN FILES" (bi-weekly / Day 26)
**Purpose:** UGC engine — subscriber stories become content.

```
SUBJECT: "I cried in a stadium full of strangers" — your stories
PREVIEW: This week, the readers take over.

THE FAN FILES — Vol. [n]

Last issue I asked: [question]. You delivered.

📬 [Reader first name, country]: "[best reply, lightly edited]"
📬 [Reader 2]: "[reply]"
📬 [Reader 3]: "[reply]"

THIS WEEK'S QUESTION:
👉 [New question — e.g. "What's the biggest World Cup upset you
   witnessed live?"] Hit reply — best answers featured next time
   (and might appear in a video).

🎬 New this week: [title] → [link]
```
