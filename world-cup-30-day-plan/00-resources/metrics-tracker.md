## 🗓️ WEEKLY REVIEW QUESTIONS (Days 07/14/21/28)
1. Which hook style won this week? (curiosity / emotion / debate / numbers)
2. Where did long-form retention dip? What segment type was it?
3. Which platform is over/under-performing vs effort?
4. What did comments ASK for? → feed next week's scripts.
5. What gets cut next week? (do less of what's flat)

## ⚖️ DECISION RULES
| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| Short > 3× channel average | 2 follow-ups on same story within 48h |
| Doc CTR < 4% after 48h | New thumbnail + retitle (once) |
| TikTok flat but Shorts strong (or vice versa) | Shift +1 daily post to the stronger platform |
| A topic flops twice | Drop the topic pillar, replace with reactive content |
| Email conversion < 15% | Rewrite landing page headline to mirror best video hook |
| Burnout risk (missed 2 days) | Cut to 1 short/day — never zero. Consistency > volume |

## 🧪 WHAT COUNTS AS "WORKING" (first 30 days)
- One format you can repeat in < 45 min that reliably clears your average
- One breakout video (>100K) you can decode and replicate
- A retention curve that's flattening week over week
- An email list that exists (any size) — that's the asset that survives the tournament
