# 🎣 THE HOOK LIBRARY
### 25+ proven opening lines & formulas, adapted for World Cup faceless content

> Rule: the hook is spoken AND on screen in the first 1.5 seconds (Shorts) or first 5 seconds (long-form). No logos. No "welcome back."

---

## A. CURIOSITY-GAP HOOKS
1. "FIFA doesn't want you to remember what happened in 2010."
2. "There's a reason this World Cup moment was never replayed on TV."
3. "He had fewer Instagram followers than your local bakery. Then the World Cup started."
4. "The stadium went quiet — for the wrong reason."
5. "One document changed the World Cup forever. Almost nobody has read it."

## B. STAKES / EMOTION HOOKS
6. "One kick. One miss. An entire country cried."
7. "One man. Six chances. One dream."
8. "This is the last time you will ever watch him at a World Cup."
9. "They were one game from elimination. Then they won everything."
10. "Imagine training your whole life — and your own federation betrays you."

## C. DEBATE / HOT-TAKE HOOKS
11. "The 48-team format will ruin the World Cup. Change my mind."
12. "FIFA just changed the World Cup forever. Here's why."
13. "You chose the wrong city to watch the World Cup."
14. "Everyone is wrong about the greatest World Cup final ever."
15. "This 'mistake' by FIFA might actually be genius."

## D. NUMBERS / LIST HOOKS
16. "3 World Cup facts that sound fake but are 100% true. Number 3 will break your brain."
17. "From 5,000 followers to 1.5 million in 48 hours. Here's how."
18. "78 matches. 16 cities. 3 countries. 1 problem nobody is talking about."
19. "5 nations that should NEVER have qualified — and what happened next."
20. "12 yards. That's all that separates legends from villains."

## E. SCIENCE / EXPLAINER HOOKS
21. "The math is rigged against goalkeepers. Here's the proof."
22. "Your brain physically changes in the 8 seconds before a penalty."
23. "Why is the penalty spot exactly 12 yards away? The answer is 130 years old."
24. "Goalkeepers dive the wrong way on purpose. Sort of."

## F. REACTIVE / NEWS-JACK HOOKS (for the daily reactive slot)
25. "What just happened at the World Cup will be talked about for years."
26. "While you were sleeping, the World Cup lost its mind."
27. "Nobody — and I mean nobody — predicted what happened tonight."
28. "This clip has 40 million views in 6 hours. Here's the story behind it."

## HOOK CONSTRUCTION FORMULAS

| Formula | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Contrast | "[Small thing] → [Huge consequence]" | "One tweet made an unknown defender world famous" |
| Forbidden | "[Authority] doesn't want you to know [thing]" | "FIFA tried to bury this scandal" |
| Time bomb | "In [timeframe], everything changed" | "In 48 hours, his life flipped upside down" |
| Direct challenge | "You're wrong about [belief]" | "You're wrong about the 48-team format" |
| Open loop | "[Event] happened. But that's not the crazy part." | Use mid-video to re-hook every 60–90s |

## RETENTION RE-HOOKS (sprinkle every 60–90 seconds in docs)
- "But that wasn't even the worst part."
- "And then it got stranger."
- "What happened next is why this video exists."
- "Remember that detail — it matters in two minutes."
- "Here's where the story everyone knows… turns out to be wrong."

## CTA LINES (one per asset, never more)
- Shorts/TikTok: "Follow — the full documentary drops [day]."
- Long-form: "Subscribe if you want the stories football tried to forget."
- Week 4+: "Free World Cup 2026 Survival Guide — link in bio/description."
