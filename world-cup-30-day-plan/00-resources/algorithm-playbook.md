# 🧠 THE ALGORITHM PLAYBOOK
### How to make YouTube, TikTok & Instagram push your World Cup content

---

## 1️⃣ YOUTUBE (Long-form + Shorts)

### What the algorithm actually optimizes for
1. **Click-through rate (CTR)** on the thumbnail/title pair — target 6–10%+.
2. **Average view duration / retention** — target 50%+ on a 7–9 min doc.
3. **Session time** — does your video lead viewers to watch MORE (yours or others)? End screens to your other docs help.
4. **Velocity** — views + engagement in the first 24–48h determine how wide it gets tested.

### Long-form rules for this strategy
- **Length:** 7–9 minutes. Long enough for mid-rolls + meaningful watch time, short enough to keep retention above 50%.
- **Title formulas:**
  - Curiosity gap: *"The World Cup Scandal FIFA Tried to Bury"*
  - Stakes + emotion: *"Messi's Last World Cup — The Final Chapter"*
  - Debate: *"The 48-Team World Cup: Genius or Disaster?"*
- **Thumbnail rules:** ONE face or object, max 3–4 words of text, high contrast (dark background + gold/red accent works for football drama). Test 2 variants with YouTube's A/B thumbnail test.
- **First 30 seconds:** cold-open with the most shocking sentence of the script. Tease the payoff ("by the end of this video you'll know why FIFA banned this from broadcast"). NEVER start with "welcome back to the channel."
- **Chapters:** add 4–6 chapters — improves retention graphs and search snippets.
- **Re-hook every 60–90 seconds:** open loops ("but that wasn't even the worst part…").

### Shorts rules
- 45–59 seconds. Loop-friendly endings (last line connects to first line).
- Hook in the **first 1.5 seconds** — text on screen + spoken word simultaneously.
- Shorts feed your long-form: pin a comment "Full documentary on the channel 👆".

### Best posting times (Europe/London base, global football audience)
- **Long-form:** Thu–Sun, 17:00–19:00 UK (catches UK evening + US lunchtime).
- **Shorts:** 12:00 and 19:00 UK daily. During match days, post within 2–4h of full-time.

## 2️⃣ TIKTOK

### What it optimizes for
1. **Watch-through % and re-watches** — the #1 signal. A 50s video watched 80%+ gets pushed hard.
2. **Shares + saves** > likes.
3. **Comment velocity** — debate bait works (polls, hot takes, "am I wrong?").
4. **Search** — TikTok is a search engine now. Say the keyword OUT LOUD in the first 3 seconds and put it in on-screen text + caption ("Tim Payne World Cup").

### TikTok rules for this strategy
- 1–3 posts/day during the tournament. Volume wins on TikTok.
- Use trending tournament sounds *under* your narration (low volume) for distribution boost.
- Reply to top comments with VIDEO replies — each is a fresh distribution lottery ticket.
- Caption formula: hook sentence + question + 3–5 hashtags (see hashtag bank).
- TikTok is FIFA's first-ever Preferred Platform in 2026 — the tournament topic itself gets boosted discovery. Ride official hashtags.

### Best posting times
- 07:00–09:00, 12:00–13:00, 19:00–22:00 UK. Match-day reactive clips: ASAP after the moment.

## 3️⃣ INSTAGRAM (Reels + Carousels + Stories)

### What it optimizes for
1. **Sends (shares via DM)** — the single biggest Reels signal. Make content people send to their football group chat.
2. **Saves** — carousels with facts/guides are save magnets.
3. **Watch time** on Reels (same as TikTok).

### Instagram rules
- **Reels:** same vertical files as TikTok (strip the TikTok watermark — Instagram demotes watermarked content).
- **Carousels:** 1 educational carousel per week (facts, rankings, guides). First slide = pure hook text. Last slide = CTA ("Save this 📌 / Full video link in bio").
- **Stories daily:** polls, emoji sliders, "this or that" — story engagement boosts your Reels reach.
- Bio link: Linktree/Beacons → YouTube channel + lead magnet.

### Best posting times
- Reels: 12:00 & 20:00 UK. Carousels: 18:00 UK. Stories: morning + during evening matches.

## 4️⃣ CROSS-PLATFORM ATOMIZATION MAP

```
1 × 7–9 min YouTube Documentary
        │
        ├── 3–5 × Shorts (best 45–59s segments, re-hooked)
        ├── 3–5 × TikToks (same files, TikTok-native captions/sounds)
        ├── 3–5 × Reels (same files, no watermark)
        ├── 1 × Educational carousel (key facts from the doc)
        ├── 1–2 × Engagement posts (poll / hot take from the doc's thesis)
        ├── 1 × Newsletter section (story summary + link)
        └── ∞ × Comment-reply videos (TikTok)
```

**Rule:** every clip must stand alone (new hook, self-contained payoff) — never "part 3 of my video" with no context.

---

## 5️⃣ THE FIRST-HOUR PROTOCOL (do this on EVERY post)

1. Publish → immediately pin a comment with a question ("Which scandal shocked you most? 👇").
2. Reply to every comment for the first 60 minutes.
3. Share to Stories (IG) and Community tab (YouTube).
4. Do NOT delete underperformers in the first 48h — Shorts/TikToks often spike late.

---

## 6️⃣ DECISION RULES (read weekly)

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| A short gets 3× your average views | Make 2 follow-ups on the same story within 48h |
| Doc retention dips at a timestamp | Note the segment type, cut it from future scripts |
| CTR < 4% after 48h | Swap thumbnail + retitle (allowed once) |
| A comment question keeps repeating | That's your next video topic |
| Reactive clip outperforms planned clip | Increase reactive slots to 2/day on match days |
