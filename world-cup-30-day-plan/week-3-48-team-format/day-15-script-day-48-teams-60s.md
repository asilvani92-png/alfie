## ✍️ TASK 1 — Documentary Script (90 min)
**Title:** The 48-Team World Cup: Genius or Disaster?
**Thumbnail text:** "GENIUS OR DISASTER?" (split design: gold vs red)

**Architecture (debate doc = different structure from story docs):**
- **Cold open:** *"FIFA took the most perfect tournament format in sports… and blew it up. This is either the bravest decision in football history — or the dumbest."*
- **Act 1 — What actually changed:** 32→48 teams · 3 host countries (first ever) · 16 cities · 104 matches · new group structure. Pure clear explanation — this earns the search traffic. → [SHORT A]
- **Act 2 — The case for DISASTER:** diluted quality, dead rubbers, player workload, calendar bloat. **Feature your Day 06 hot-take comment section** ("when I said this last week, you said…" — screenshot-style narration). → [SHORT B]
- **Act 3 — The case for GENIUS:** 16 new nations get the dream (tie back to Day 11's Country Spotlight!), new markets, more Tim Paynes, more upsets — and the money funds grassroots football. → [SHORT C]
- **Payoff:** *"Here's the truth: we said the same about 24 teams in 1982 and 32 in 1998. The World Cup has never been ruined by getting bigger. It's been ruined by getting boring — and 2026 is anything but."*
- **CTA:** *"Verdict in the comments: genius or disaster. One word. Go."*

> 🧠 Structure note: presenting BOTH sides earns trust + doubles the arguing audience. The one-word CTA produces massive low-friction comment counts.
