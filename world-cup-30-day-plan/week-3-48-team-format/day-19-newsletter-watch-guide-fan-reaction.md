## ⚡ TASK 2 — Fan-Reaction Short (60 min)
**Title:** "The Reaction That Broke the Internet"
**Hook:** *"This fan's reaction has [X] million views. Here's the story behind it."*
**How (faceless + rights-safe):** do NOT re-upload someone's clip. **Narrate the story around** the viral moment using stock crowd footage: who, where, what happened in the match at that second, why the planet related to it. The story-behind-the-story format stands on its own.
**Pick:** whatever fan moment is genuinely viral this week (there will be one — crying fan, ecstatic granddad, stadium-wide limbs). Reactive freshness matters: post same-day as the trend if possible.
**Loop:** *"The World Cup isn't about football. It's about THIS. Follow for the stories behind the moments."*
