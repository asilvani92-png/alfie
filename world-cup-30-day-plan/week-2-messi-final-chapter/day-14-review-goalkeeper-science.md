**Hook:** *"The math is rigged against goalkeepers. Here's the proof."*
**Beats (55s):**
1. Hook.
2. The ball crosses the line in ~0.4 seconds. Human reaction + dive takes longer. Keepers MUST move before the kick.
3. So they guess — and game theory says shooters exploit predictable guessing.
4. The twist: statistically, keepers who simply stand still save more central penalties… but they dive anyway.
5. Why? **Action bias** — diving and losing *looks* better than standing and losing.
6. Loop: *"Penalties aren't a lottery. They're psychology. And there's more where this came from — follow."*
