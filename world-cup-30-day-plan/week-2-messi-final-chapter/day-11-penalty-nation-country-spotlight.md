## ⚡ TASK 1 — "The Penalty That Changed a Nation" (60 min)
**Hook:** *"One kick. One miss. An entire country cried."*
**Pick one documented story**, e.g.:
- Baggio, USA '94 — the greatest player of the tournament, the miss, the image of him standing alone
- Or Ghana 2010 — Gyan's crossbar after the handball on the line, a continent's heartbreak

**Structure (55s):** HOOK → 15s of context (what was at stake) → the moment, slowed down narratively ("the run-up took four seconds; the silence after lasted years") → aftermath (what it did to the player/nation) → loop: *"Football isn't a game. It's a memory a whole country carries. Follow for more."*
**MPT:** `goalkeeper dive`, `slow motion ball`, `empty stadium`, `fans crying` · emotional piano → silence at the miss.
**Note:** this short doubles as the bridge to Day 14's goalkeeper-science short and Week 3's psychology angles. You're building a connected universe.

## 🌍 TASK 2 — Country Spotlight (45 min)
**Post:** *"This tiny nation qualified for the first time ever. Here's their story."*
- Pick a genuine 2026 debutant (check the news for current first-time qualifiers — e.g., a small nation whose qualification was this cycle's fairytale).
- **Format:** IG carousel (6 slides: hook → population/size context → the qualifying moment → the hero → what it means → "save + follow") AND a 45s narrated short version.
- **Why:** emotional education = saves; diaspora communities share national pride content at extreme rates — a small nation's entire online community can pile onto one post.
