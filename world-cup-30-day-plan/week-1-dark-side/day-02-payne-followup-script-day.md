## ✍️ TASK 2 — Write the Documentary Script (75–90 min)
**Title:** The Dark Side of World Cup History
**Working thumbnail text:** "FIFA BURIED THIS"

**Script architecture (follow `moneyprinterturbo-workflow.md` structure):**
- **Cold open (0:00–0:25):** *"In 2010, an entire national team sat on a bus and refused to train at the World Cup. That's not even in the top three darkest stories of this tournament."*
- **Act 1 — The Mutiny (France 2010):** coach vs star, expulsion, the strike on the bus, national outrage back home. End loop: *"But a strike is nothing compared to what money makes people do."* → mark [SHORT A]
- **Act 2 — The Money (FIFA corruption era):** bidding scandals, arrests, the machinery behind hosting rights. End loop: *"And sometimes the dark side isn't in boardrooms. It's on the pitch."* → mark [SHORT B]
- **Act 3 — On-pitch darkness:** match-fixing cases / disgrace matches in WC history. → mark [SHORT C]
- **Payoff:** what changed, what didn't — *"2026 is the biggest World Cup ever. Which means the stakes for the dark side have never been higher."*
- **CTA:** *"Which story shocked you most? Comments. And subscribe — these are the stories football tried to forget."*

⚠️ **Compliance note:** stick to widely reported, documented facts; use "allegedly" where charges weren't proven. No copyrighted match footage — stock B-roll only.
